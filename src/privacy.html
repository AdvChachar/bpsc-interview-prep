Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 May 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how BPSC Interview Prep ("we", "our", "the Service") collects, uses, discloses, and protects your personal information when you use our interview preparation platform.

2. Information We Collect

Account Information: When you sign up, we collect your email address and a securely hashed password via Supabase authentication.

Profile Information: You may voluntarily provide your name, educational background (degree, university, graduation year), district/hometown, post applied for, strengths and weaknesses, and other details used to personalize your interview preparation.

Progress Data: We store your session progress, completed questions, mock interview scores, and checklist items to sync your experience across devices.

Authentication Data: If you use Google or Facebook sign-in, we receive basic profile information from those providers (email, name) as governed by their respective privacy policies.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To personalize AI-generated interview content based on your background
  • To save and sync your progress across sessions
  • To improve the Service based on aggregate usage patterns
  • To communicate with you about Service updates or changes

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase (hosted on AWS infrastructure). We implement industry-standard security measures including encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest. Passwords are never stored in plain text. You own your data — we do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

5. Third-Party Services

Supabase: Authentication and database services. See Supabase Privacy Policy.

Mistral AI: Used to generate personalized interview content. Queries are sent anonymously. See Mistral Privacy Policy.

Netlify: Hosting and deployment. See Netlify Privacy Policy.

Google & Facebook OAuth: Used only for authentication. We receive only what you authorize (email, name).

6. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may delete your account and associated data at any time by contacting us. Progress data stored locally in your browser (localStorage) can be cleared via browser settings.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Export your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise these rights, contact us through the Service.

8. Cookies & Local Storage

We use browser localStorage (not cookies) to cache your progress for offline resilience. No tracking cookies or analytics scripts are used. Supabase uses necessary cookies for authentication sessions.

9. Children's Privacy

The Service is intended for users preparing for BPSC examinations, typically adults. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us for deletion.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be notified via email or a notice on the Service. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.

11. Governing Law

This Privacy Policy is governed by the laws of the Islamic Republic of Pakistan, specifically the Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act 2016 (PECA) and applicable data protection regulations.

12. Contact

For privacy-related inquiries, contact us through the Service or at the contact information provided on the website.
