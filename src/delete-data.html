How to Delete Your Account & Data

You have the right to delete your account and all associated personal data from BPSC Interview Prep. Below are the available methods:

Method 1: Self-Service via Supabase

You can delete your data by contacting us directly. Send an email from the address associated with your account with the subject line "Delete My Data" and we will process the request within 7 business days.

Method 2: Email Request

Send an email to chachar1819@gmail.com with:

  • Subject: Account Deletion Request
  • Your registered email address
  • A brief message confirming you want your account and all associated data permanently deleted

Method 3: WhatsApp Request

You can also request data deletion via WhatsApp at +92 334 2412636. Include your registered email address in the message.

What Gets Deleted

  • Your account credentials (email, auth metadata)
  • Your profile information (name, degree, university, district, etc.)
  • Your session progress, completed questions, and scores
  • Mock interview results and answers
  • Any other personal data stored in our database

What is NOT Deleted

  • Anonymized/aggregated usage statistics (not linked to you personally)
  • Data we are legally required to retain under applicable Pakistani law

Processing Time

Deletion requests are processed within 7 business days. You will receive a confirmation email once the deletion is complete. After deletion, you will no longer be able to sign in or access any data associated with your account.

Data Backup Retention

Copies of your data may remain in our encrypted backups for up to 30 days after deletion, after which they are automatically overwritten. These backups are inaccessible for any operational purpose.

Contact

If you have questions about data deletion, contact us at chachar1819@gmail.com or via WhatsApp at +92 334 2412636.
